# Receipts and sources (fetched abstracts)

**A Cyclic Model of the Universe.** Steinhardt PJ, Turok NN. arXiv:hep-th/0111030 (2001).
> "We propose a cosmological model in which the universe undergoes an endless sequence of
> cosmic epochs each beginning with a 'bang' and ending in a 'crunch.' The temperature and
> density are finite at each transition from crunch to bang... each cycle includes a period
> of slow accelerated expansion (as recently observed) followed by slow contraction."

**The Cyclic Model Simplified.** Steinhardt PJ, Turok NN. arXiv:astro-ph/0404480 (2004).
> "The Cyclic Model attempts to resolve the homogeneity, isotropy, and flatness problems
> and generate a nearly scale-invariant spectrum of fluctuations during a period of slow
> contraction that precedes a bounce to an expanding phase."
