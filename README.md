# Cyclic Accumulation — what survives the turn of the aeon (Turok program)

Third-party questions and constructions for Neil Turok's cyclic-cosmology program: the live problem of entropy across aeons (what accumulates, what resets), framed through the equation chain MIND-ZOE LIFE = INFINITUDES OF NEGENTROPIC ACTIVITY; i becomes I by accumulation of states and memory; I = N (Information IS Negentropy). Whether the conformal rescaling between cycles preserves any accumulated record — a physical ledger across aeons — is the kind of question this repository exists to formalize.
